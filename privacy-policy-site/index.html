1. Summary in plain language

We know most people won't read every section below, so here is the short version:

  • 101matik is a scoreboard/tally app for card and tile games (such as "101"/Okey). It works fully offline.
  • We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers ourselves.
  • Everything you type into the App (player names, team names, scores) is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server we control.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob and offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") processed by Google Play Billing. These Google services may collect limited technical data as described below.
  • You can delete all locally stored data at any time using the "Reset" button in the App, or by uninstalling the App.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide (stored locally only)

The App lets you enter player names, team names, hand-by-hand scores, and game settings (number of hands, penalty rules, theme preference, etc.). This information is saved using your device's local browser storage (localStorage) inside the App's embedded WebView component. It is not transmitted to us, not linked to your identity, and not stored on any server operated by us — we simply have no access to it.

2.2 Information collected automatically by third-party SDKs

The App integrates two Google SDKs (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Google Play Billing) to display ads and process the optional purchase. These SDKs may automatically collect and process technical data such as:

2.3 What we do NOT collect

  • No account registration, no email or phone number collection by us
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location access
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is currently integrated by us
  • No data is sold to third parties

3. How we use information

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we do not "use" it in the traditional sense. The locally stored game data is used exclusively to:

  • Display and calculate scores while you play
  • Remember your settings and theme between sessions
  • Generate the optional shareable result image you can save/send to friends

Data collected by Google's SDKs (see 2.2) is used by Google to serve relevant ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and process your purchase, as described in Google's own policies linked below.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

101matik is free to use and partly supported by advertising served through Google AdMob. Two ad formats are used:

  • Rewarded interstitial ad — shown (with your explicit on-screen consent, "Yes/No") before starting a new game. Watching it in full unlocks the scoreboard for that game session.
  • Interstitial ad — an optional, skippable ad that may appear when a game finishes or when you export/share a result image. It is never shown while you are actively entering scores.

AdMob and its partner ad networks may use the Advertising ID and other technical signals described in section 2.2 to select and measure ads, and — where required by your region's law (e.g. the EEA, UK, or California) — to request your consent for personalized advertising via a consent prompt. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and adjust your ad personalization settings, at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings (manage ad personalization)

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers one optional, one-time, non-consumable in-app product: "Remove Ads" (product ID: remove_ads). Purchasing it permanently disables the ads described in section 4 on that device account. The entire purchase flow — including price display, payment method, billing, receipts, and refunds — is handled by Google Play under Google's own Privacy Policy and Play Terms of Service. We only store a local flag on your device indicating whether the purchase was completed, so the App knows not to show ads again.

7. Sharing and disclosure of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information. Data may only be disclosed:

  • To Google, as an inherent part of using the AdMob and Play Billing SDKs described above;
  • If required to comply with a valid legal obligation, court order, or governmental request;
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our users, or the public, where permitted by law.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data (player names, scores, settings) remains on your device only for as long as you keep it there. You can delete it at any time via:

  • The in-app "Reset" button on the setup screen, which immediately clears all locally stored game data; or
  • Clearing the App's storage from your device's Android system settings; or
  • Uninstalling the App entirely.

We do not retain any copy of this data ourselves, since it never leaves your device through our own systems. Data retained by Google (ad and billing telemetry) is governed by Google's own retention practices as described in their privacy policy.

9. Security

Because game data stays on-device, its security depends primarily on your own device's security (lock screen, OS updates, etc.). We take reasonable measures in how the App is built to avoid unnecessary data exposure, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security for data processed by third-party services such as Google.

10. Children's privacy

101matik is a general-audience scoring utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at kawinsegaming@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to address it. Note that Google AdMob provides tools for developers to restrict ad content and data collection for child-directed apps; this App is not configured as child-directed because it does not target children.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding data processed by the third-party services used in this App:

  • Access & portability — request a copy of data Google holds about you via your Google Account.
  • Deletion — reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Android Settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), or reset in-app data via the "Reset" button described in section 8.
  • Opt out of personalized ads — via Google Ads Settings or your device's ad personalization toggle.
  • Withdraw consent — if you were shown a consent prompt (e.g. in the EEA/UK), you can change your choice at any time through that prompt or your device settings.

12. GDPR (European Economic Area & UK users)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, Google (and its ad technology partners) may act as independent data controllers for the data described in section 2.2, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR. You may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, and the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we ourselves do not collect or store personal data on any server, most of these rights are best exercised directly with Google via the links in sections 4 and 11.

We do not sell personal information. To exercise choices related to advertising identifiers used by Google AdMob, use the opt-out tools listed in section 11, or contact us at kawinsegaming@gmail.com.

16. Offline functionality

The core scorekeeping feature of 101matik works fully offline — you can create games, enter scores, and view results without any internet connection, since all game logic and data run locally on your device. An internet connection is only used opportunistically to load ads or process the optional purchase; if no connection is available, the App still lets you play without interruption.
